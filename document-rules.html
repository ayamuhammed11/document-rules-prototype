Document Rules

Create customized rules that decide which documents a merchant must submit to proceed with the onboarding application — driven by conditions such as entity type, owner nationality, industry, bank, module, service, and channel. A rule with no conditions applies to every applicant.

Requirement Rules

Each rule links one or more document types to conditions. When the merchant or salesperson's selections during onboarding match the conditions, the documents are displayed and required to proceed. Empty conditions = required for everyone.
